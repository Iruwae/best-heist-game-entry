# GILDED HOUR — a midnight museum heist

One night. Three galleries. Three pieces. One getaway.

Marlowe slips into the museum after dark to steal the Gilded Ledger, the
Star Sapphire and the Brass Key — then reach the green getaway door before
the wardens or the wall cameras raise the alarm to full.

- **Top-down stealth on one screen:** four patrolling wardens with amber
  vision cones, three sweeping red cameras, and an alarm meter that climbs
  whenever you are seen — and falls again when you break sight.
- **Laser grids in the middle gallery:** two red beams sweep up and down —
  cross between sweeps, because a beam that touches you feeds the alarm,
  and smoke hides you from eyes, not from light.
- **Several routes per room:** two doorways through every partition wall,
  display cases and statues to hide behind.
- **Tools at a cost:** smoke pellets (2 coins) blind every cone, EMP
  charges (3 coins) knock the cameras dark. Coins are scattered on the
  floor — spend them or save them.
- **A fair caught moment:** fill the alarm and the wardens reset the room,
  but your loot stays and the clock keeps its +30s penalty.
- **Original art and sound:** flat night-museum palette, gold loot,
  velvet shadow — and every sound synthesized live in the browser.

Play it: open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome. No network, no build
step. WASD or arrows to move, Shift to sneak, Q for smoke, E for EMP,
M to mute.
